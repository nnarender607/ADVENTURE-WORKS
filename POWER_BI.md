# Power BI Interactive Dashboard - Adventure Works

## 📊 Overview
The interactive Power BI dashboard suite serves as a comprehensive analytical reporting solution for the Adventure Works project, encompassing dedicated views for **Sales Dashboard**, **Production Dashboard**, and **Profit Dashboard**. Equipped with dynamic global filters (Year, Quarter, Month, Country, and Subcategory) and macro-level KPI scorecards, it allows stakeholders to seamlessly track and analyze overall corporate performance.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Sections & Key Insights

### **1. Sales Dashboard**
* **Executive Summary KPIs:** Tracks overall financial health with a total Sales Amount of **₹29.36M**, Total Profit of **₹12.08M**, Total Orders of **60.40K**, and Production Cost of **₹17.28M**.
* **Year-Wise Sales (Bar Chart):** Displays annual revenue progression across multiple years, peaking notably with **₹16.35M** in 2023.
* **Quarter-Wise Sales (Pie Chart):** Illustrates revenue distribution across calendar quarters, highlighting top-performing periods like Q1 (**₹9.11M** / **31.02%**) and Q3 (**₹7.64M** / **26.02%**).
* **Month-Wise Sales (Line Chart):** Maps seasonal sales velocity throughout the calendar year, charting trends from January through December.
* **Sales and Production Cost by Region (Combination Chart):** Dual-axis visual comparing regional performance and cost distribution across sales territories like Australia, Southwest, and Northwest.
* **Category-Wise Sales (Tree Map / Breakdown):** Visualizes revenue breakdown across product categories and sub-categories (led by the *Mountain-200* series).
* **Top 10 Products & Customers by Sales:** Dynamic ranking charts highlighting high-value product lines and top enterprise customers.

### **2. Production Dashboard**
* **Production Cost Overview KPIs:** Evaluates manufacturing and operational overhead against macro metrics, maintaining total figures of **₹29.36M** sales, **₹12.08M** profit, and **₹17.28M** production cost.
* **Year-Wise Production Cost:** Tracks annual manufacturing expenditures and scaling costs across operational years.
* **Quarter-Wise Production Cost:** Breaks down production allocations across fiscal quarters (showing Q1 at **₹5.35M** / **30.94%**).
* **Month-Wise Production Cost:** Maps monthly manufacturing trends and resource deployment across the year.
* **Sales and Production Cost by Region:** Correlates regional sales output directly against localized production expenses.
* **Category-Wise & Top 10 Products/Customers by Production Cost:** Detailed rankings identifying cost-intensive product segments (such as the *Mountain-200* series) and key customer operational volumes.
